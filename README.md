# Overview
Simmilar to BetterThanMending - this mod allows you to repair your equipment, with mending enchantment on it, by using experience you have right here, and right now, instead of requiring you to passively earn it.

Although it has its differences to BetterThanMending.


![Usage](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/a83cb52f3c88222286fb2f8c96a128a0536ca35b.gif)


# Features:
1. Feedback sound whilst mending
2. Sneak + Right click the item you want to repair with the secondary cost in your offhand.
3. Configuration menu.

## Configurables
1. Level limit, which does not allow you to repair your equipment without anvil, if your level less than the one you've configured.
2. Option to require a specific amount of configurable non-placeable items in your offhand to do the actual repairment. (lapis by default)
3. limit - how much you can actively repair your gear
4. Repair cost
5. Repair amount
6. Option, which will not allow players to repair their equipment passively, if they haven't reached a specific level.


# The goal
To bring more sense in experience bar, which right now is somewhat useless, I am trying to make little and simple mods, which should together make it actually usefull, for example by allowing certain features to be used from a specific level. in my opinion experience should be a resource, which you should also manage, simmilar to inventory, hunger and health, not "the thing required for enchantments"
